**Teaching source:** Hongkai Zhang and Bin Jiang, *Living Structure + AI: Four Hands-On Exercises for Exploring and Assessing Wholeness*, user-supplied 11-page PDF, especially pp. 1, 5, 10–11. Its VAS examples motivate joint and separate analysis. It is not redistributed. Its figure patterns are illustrative, not evidence that VAS establishes wholeness or that all future scans show the same pattern. Its screenshots include historical quota information; the tutorial deliberately does not promise a fixed number of free scans.

**Local research safeguards:** panel identity and position swaps; frozen AOI geometry; hash-based provenance; no generative enhancement; distinction between local ROI and native AOI; no selection of favorable categories after inspection; native-versus-composite labeling; checkpoint recovery. These are methodological recommendations, not claims of official 3M upload requirements.

**Previously observed interface behavior:** the Re-analyze warning and category examples come from the author’s prior workflow records, not from a newly submitted scan in this release. The exact category descriptions and destructive-action warnings must be checked in the current interface before use. No live scan was purchased or submitted to prepare this tutorial.

**Earlier protocol:** an older near/far workflow specified Other, separate scans, a 2.35× digital crop, building-envelope ROIs and particular R thresholds. Those settings belonged to that experiment. They are not generalized into default VAS requirements. Current comparison work additionally motivates the distinction between native AOI probability, hotspot probability and color-derived coverage.

**Attribution:** Tutorial prepared by Shuyuun Wang. Supervisor: Bin Jiang. Handbook authors: Hongkai Zhang and Bin Jiang. No supervisory approval, coauthorship, institutional endorsement or 3M endorsement is asserted.
