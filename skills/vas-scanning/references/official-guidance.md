# Official guidance and local recommendations

Verified 24 October 2026. Recheck the linked pages and current UI if the service changes.

- [VAS FAQs](https://vas.3m.com/faqs): RGB JPG, PNG and PDF; recommended minimum 600 × 600 pixels; first-glance scope roughly 3–5 seconds. Hotspot/AOI percentages refer to seeing somewhere within the stated region, and gaze points are predictions. VAS is not a complete substitute for eye tracking.
- [3M Support](https://www.3m.com/3M/en_US/visual-attention-software-us/support/): smaller images may be automatically resized and results affected. Keep input dimensions/resolution consistent in comparisons. Prefer realistic, wider landscape photographs. This is not permission to distort the scene, rotate its contents or invent detail.
- [How it Works](https://vas.3m.com/howitworks): content-specific algorithms; upload, select content type, set AOIs if needed, analyze, preserve results. Three basic reports; AOIs enable two additional focused reports.

The minimum recommendation concerns the uploaded visual. For montages also inspect each source panel: a large canvas or large blank border can hide very small source content. Do not mislabel a per-panel research check as an official 600-pixel rule. No maximum upload size or mandatory DPI is asserted here because neither was established in the verified sources.

Recommended local practice: preserve originals and credits, freeze preprocessing and comparison rules, report confounds, keep native evidence, and distinguish predictions from conclusions about aesthetics. The official guides do not establish a VAS beauty score, an architectural category for every possible input, or validated coherence thresholds.

Prior category examples were checked locally in October 2026. Confirm labels and descriptions in the current UI; an old example is not a current specification. Similarly, prior Re-analyze behavior is destructive and should never be assumed safe without checking and preserving outputs.
